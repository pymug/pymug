PyCon US was an amazing experience for me. It was my first time visiting the United States, and everyone I met was very welcoming. I got to meet people from many different Python communities and even had the chance to talk with some well-known members of the Python world. Volunteering at the conference made the experience even more special, as I was able to help the event while meeting people from all over the world. I also attended many interesting talks, including one about building a PDF generator using Python generators.

PyMUG, the Mauritius Python User Group, is a great place for people to get started with Python and become part of the community. The meetups are welcoming and give everyone a chance to learn, share ideas, and contribute. They also help people build confidence by speaking, presenting, and taking part in community projects and larger events like PyCon US.
